In my local workflow, I’ve basically switched from Git to [jj](https://jj-vcs.github.io/jj/) for a few months now. The overall experience is steadier and smoother than I expected. Many of those small cleanup steps I “knew I could do but never bothered” now feel low-cost.

More importantly for agent collaboration: when multiple AI agents and humans are editing code together, jj’s operational model makes it easier for me to keep history clean, reduce branch-management overhead, and revisit or adjust my thinking midstream. For me, it genuinely fits this era better than traditional Git.

If you’re also rethinking version control, check out my full write-up: [jj for Agent Era](https://onevcat.com/2026/03/jj-for-agent-era/). It covers the practical details and the reasoning behind the choice in more depth.
