A Tesla gives me a lot more to tinker with than most cars. With [FleetAPI](https://developer.tesla.com/docs/fleet-api/getting-started/what-is-fleet-api) and [Telemetry](https://developer.tesla.com/docs/fleet-api/fleet-telemetry), plus the $10 in monthly credits from Tesla Developer, I can collect vehicle data from end to end, including trip routes, live speed, and battery status. Even more interesting are [virtual keys](https://developer.tesla.com/docs/fleet-api/virtual-keys/overview), which let me pair the car with a private server and control some of its functions through an API. Combined with the [catgirl team](https://ai.onev.cat/articles/zh/2026-02-22-openclaw-multi-agent-team/) I set up earlier, this means I can now chat with an agent on Discord to research routes, find parking, and check the surrounding area, then have it send a destination to the car or adjust the climate control based on when I plan to leave. I am having a great time with it. It feels less like replacing my car and more like buying a giant tech toy.
