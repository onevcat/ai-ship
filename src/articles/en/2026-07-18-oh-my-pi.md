---
title: "Why I Made Oh My Pi My Main Harness"
date: "2026-07-18"
tools: ["omp"]
category: "Work"
---

I recently switched my main harness to Oh My Pi. It does not lock the model in: within the same workflow, I can choose the model myself instead of inheriting defaults such as “Codex means GPT, Claude Code means Opus.” Models and the environments that call them evolve on different schedules. I would rather choose them separately.

I have also been cleaning up a habit of mine. Hooks and skills that only run in one harness are convenient until I change environments. Then they become baggage. From now on, I will favor general commands and scripts instead of putting more into a specific harness. The next time I switch tools, I will not have to haul migration debt along with me.

Plain Pi is a little too bare for me. OMP adds the layer I need: there is room to customize it and keep building on top of it. I like a harness I can gradually shape around my own habits.
